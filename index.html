Where this comes from. The same Laguna Beach restaurant list as the Happy Hours page, plus a search for family spots and kids' specials, checked in October 2026. Kids' deals also come from the OC Parent Guide's Kids Eat Free list; deals a chain doesn't post on its own site are marked to confirm when you order. Prices are menu prices; tax and tip are extra.
Freshness. Each card shows when it was last checked. Cards not checked in 90 days get a "May be out of date" tag. The list is rechecked automatically on the 1st of each month.
Hours and happy hours. Opening hours come from each restaurant's site or ordering page where possible (Oct 2026); some come from listings and are noted on the card. Happy hour times come from the Laguna Happy Hours page.
Labels. Verified: the restaurant's own website or own online ordering showed it in 2026, or two independent 2026 sources agree. Likely: one recent credible source. Needs confirmation: older or conflicting sources. Prices taken from delivery apps or third-party price sites are marked on the item, since they usually run higher than in the restaurant.
Ratings. Google ratings come from Wanderlog, which mirrors Google's listing data. Yelp links open Yelp directly.
